The collider

This is Warps' META mode in the browser, using the firmware's own code. ALGORITHM goes through the same pot curve. The six cross-modulation algorithms, how neighbouring algorithms blend, the skewed TIMBRE and the handover to the vocoder are ported line for line from modulator.cc. So are its lookup tables: the crossfade, the bipolar fold and the pot curve are embedded here. Move ALGORITHM and watch the sidebands appear.

What is simplified. Warps oversamples ×6 around the cross-modulation and runs each input through a soft-saturating VCA. This runs at 96 kHz without oversampling, and each input is just scaled by level², the drive Warps uses. Its internal oscillators are band-limited, and these are not. The vocoder is not simulated: past about 0.68 on the algorithm scale the output fades to the modulator, as on the module, and above 0.73 this page stops.

What it does

This is Warps, Emilie Gillet's meta-modulator for Mutable Instruments, in Matthias Puech's Parasites build, compiled for the Alchemy Lab. The engine is warps::Modulator from mqtthiqs/parasites at 32fa66f, unmodified apart from one marked fix. It runs at Warps' own 96 kHz, in 60-frame blocks.

META mode is stock Warps. ALGORITHM sweeps crossfade, fold, analog ring modulation, digital ring modulation, XOR, comparator and vocoder, and TIMBRE is the parameter of whichever algorithm you're on. Parasites adds eight more modes: Doppler, Fold, Chebyshev, Frequency shifter, Bitcrusher, Comparator, Vocoder and Delay.

Nothing on J1? Pick an internal carrier with CARRIER (sine, triangle or saw), as with Warps' button. LEVEL 1 then sets its pitch, J5 adds 1 V per octave, and TUNE on the Setup page transposes it. Whatever is on J1 becomes through-zero FM into that oscillator.

Carrier in on J1, modulator in on J2, out on J9, and aux on J10. With the internal carrier on, the aux output is that oscillator.

Buttons

B1 · CARRIER. A tap steps CARRIER, which is Warps' button: external, sine, triangle, saw. In Doppler it sets the room size, and in Delay the loop type. The stored value moves, so the ring follows and the change is saved, and the CARRIER pot has to catch its new value before it takes over again.

B2 · MODE. A tap steps MODE through META and Parasites' eight. It stands down while B3 is held, so the B2 + B3 Settings chord never steps the mode on the way in.

B3 · SETUP. Hold it to show the Setup page: IN GAIN and TUNE. The other four Setup pots do nothing. Hold B2 + B3 for two seconds to open Settings.

META: where each algorithm sits on ALGORITHM

The pot goes through Warps' lut_pot_curve, which gives each algorithm a flat spot. Between flats, neighbouring algorithms crossfade. Internally the value is multiplied by 8: 0 is crossfade, 1 fold, 2 analog ring mod, 3 digital ring mod, 4 XOR, 5 comparator. From about 0.68 the output fades toward the vocoder, which takes over completely above 0.73.

Level CV adds to the knob here; it isn't a VCA. On a real Warps, a cable in a LEVEL jack turns it into a VCA (drive = pot² × CV × 1.6). The module finds out with a probe signal on the jack's normalling. The Lab can't sense a cable, so this firmware always behaves as if nothing is patched (drive = level²), and CV adds to the knob. On a real Warps the LEVEL 1 CV jack is also the V/Oct input. Here V/Oct has its own jack, J5.

Reading the lights

B1 shows CARRIER in Warps' button colours: dim for external (or tiny, or open), then green, yellow and red.

B2 is the mode's colour (see the table above). It turns red when the audio callback averages over 80 % CPU.

B3 has a dim Setup tint.

P1 at boot. For 2.5 s after power-up, the P1 ring shows the previous session's worst audio-block load. Green is under 75 %, amber under 90 %, and red is 90 % or more. A single dim blue dot means there's no data yet. At 96 kHz this is the number to watch, most of all in Vocoder mode.

Firmware on the card

Put warps_alchemy.bin in the /alchemy folder on the SD card, beside the other Alchemy firmwares, and pick it on Settings page 2:

  • P1 FILE picks the file.
  • P2 FLASH writes it: turn below 85 %, then past 95 %.
  • P3 DFU does the same gesture, and reboots into the bootloader.

The firmware picker is the same in every mi-alchemy firmware and in Mark, Belt and Smack.

Where it saves. Warps keeps its working state in its own QSPI preset slot, 7 (counting from 0), and saves it about five seconds after the last change, once no button is held. Across the card: Warps 7, Clouds 8, Elements 9, Marbles 10, Plaits 11, Smack 12, Mark 13, Belt 14, Relay 15. That leaves slots 0–6 for presets you save by hand.

First boot after a flash: MODE is META and CARRIER is external, so a fresh module is stock Warps on its jacks. IN GAIN is at 0 dB and TUNE at 0. The other PLAY pots take whatever position they're in.

Status

Proven on a computer

  • Builds to a 393 KB image, 80 % of the SRAM region. The Modulator (123 KB) sits in the Lab's D2 RAM.
  • Digital ring mod of 1000 Hz × 200 Hz gives the 800 Hz and 1200 Hz sidebands, with the inputs suppressed by more than 20 orders of magnitude.
  • Every mode with all four CARRIER states runs for a second at 96 kHz. The output stays finite and never sticks at full scale. Delay runs in all four loop types, which exercises the fix below.
  • The internal sine measures 370.0 Hz at LEVEL 1 noon and 740.0 Hz with +1 V on J5.

Not yet tested on the module

  • Nothing has been flashed or heard.
  • CPU at 96 kHz on the Cortex-M7. It should fit, since Warps ran this at 96 kHz on a 168 MHz STM32F405, but it's unmeasured. Watch the Vocoder.
  • Input level. IN GAIN starts at 0 dB, and the Lab's codec scaling against Warps' input range is unknown. Fold, drive and the comparator all respond to level.

Left out

  • The patched-cable probe (see Jacks), stock Warps' hidden frequency shifter (Parasites has its own), and Warps' own calibration. The Lab's factory CV calibration is used instead.
  • Stock Warps as a separate tenth mode. META is already stock Warps' algorithms.
  • Launchpad and USB audio. This v0.1 is HostLink only, and it never changes the card's USB setting, so the other firmwares keep theirs.

One fix to the vendored code. Parasites' delay never wrapped its read index above the buffer size. ASan found it while Warps was being ported to the Daisy Patch (Meld), and it's marked in the source and listed in vendor/VENDOR.md.
